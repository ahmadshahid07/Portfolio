About Me

I am a developer and small-business owner, passionate about building practical digital products and modern software solutions. My interests include full-stack web development, mobile application development, AI-powered applications, database management, and digital product development.

I work with technologies such as HTML, CSS, JavaScript, React, Next.js, Node.js, Java, C++, SQL, Flutter, and Dart, along with database and backend platforms including MySQL, PostgreSQL, Supabase, and Firebase. I am continuously improving my skills in responsive UI design, REST API integration, authentication, database architecture, and cloud-based application development.

My projects and product concepts focus on solving real-world problems through technology. These include Trace-it, a campus Lost and Found platform; GrowthPilot AI, an AI-powered digital marketing platform; Rankora, an SEO analysis and optimization tool; and Skyth's, a leather wallet e-commerce brand. I am also exploring mobile and AI-driven solutions in property technology, cricket analysis, and emergency communication.

Beyond development, I am interested in entrepreneurship, product strategy, user experience, and turning ideas into useful digital products. My goal is to develop secure, scalable, user-friendly applications while continuously learning modern technologies and building solutions with real-world value.

I welcome opportunities to collaborate on web applications, mobile apps, AI integrations, and innovative software projects.
